# Design System: Atelier Wisal

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "Atelier Wisal"**

Wisal is a living invitation atelier. The invitation demonstrates the product before the supporting tools explain it: a large porcelain invitation rests over lilac silk, aubergine language stays calm and direct, and one chartreuse action gives the composition its pulse. Celebration comes from material, scale, and craft rather than ornamental wedding clichés.

The shared shell is pale, tactile, bilingual, and composed. Marketing may be expressive; studio, dashboard, guest-management, and administration surfaces lower the material intensity while retaining porcelain, lilac, and aubergine. The invitation collection is deliberately independent: six authored worlds share a reliable guest journey but own their palette, composition, image treatment, and opening moment.

**Key Characteristics:**

- Continuous pale lilac through the first fold, with porcelain surfaces and real silk depth.
- Aubergine type, hairline structure, and chartreuse reserved for decisive action or selected state.
- A large invitation object as primary product proof, supported by semantic preview controls.
- Dense operational information presented calmly without losing hierarchy.
- English and Arabic treated as equal compositions with native type metrics and direction.
- Six invitation worlds preserving their own materials inside one dependable guest flow.

## Colors

The shell is a lilac atelier with porcelain light, aubergine ink, and a scarce chartreuse signal; invitation colors never leak into the surrounding product shell.

### Primary

- **Atelier Lilac:** The continuous hero, final call-to-action, and material-story field.
- **Atelier Aubergine:** Primary text, dark feature fields, footer ground, and authoritative operational actions.
- **Atelier Chartreuse:** The high-energy signal for primary creation, selected previews, and key journey icons.

### Secondary

- **Lilac Soft:** A quiet raised field behind collections and supporting regions.
- **Porcelain:** The page ground, header glass, cards, and invitation-like surfaces.

### Neutral

- **Aubergine Soft:** Supporting copy, metadata, and non-primary navigation.
- **Atelier Line:** Hairline separation for header, workflow, plans, and operational groupings.
- **White:** High-contrast text on aubergine and clean cards where the implementation uses pure white.

### Invitation Worlds

- **Élan Editorial / أناقة التحرير:** Warm cotton field and paper, oxblood action, brown-black ink, antique gold, and muted cocoa.
- **Garden Reverie / حُلم الحديقة:** Sage-beige field, botanical paper, oxblood action, earthen ink, aged gold, and moss-grey support.
- **Glass Moon / قمر زجاجي:** Black-plum field, deep translucent paper, pearl ink, blue-black action, warm lunar gold, and pale muted copy.
- **Gilded Promise / الوعد المُذهّب:** Burgundy field, cream paper, wine action, dark garnet ink, champagne gold, and dusty rose support.
- **Still / سُكون:** Warm white field, clean paper, near-black ink, wine action, restrained bronze, and neutral grey support.
- **Afterglow Première / وهج الغروب:** Black-cherry field, dark paper, ivory ink, garnet action, sunset gold, and warm grey support.

**The Shell and Artifact Rule.** Lilac, porcelain, aubergine, and chartreuse define the product shell; each invitation world remains an independent authored artifact.

**The One Signal Rule.** Chartreuse marks a primary next step or selected state. It does not become a decorative fill across large surfaces.

## Typography

**Display Font:** Cormorant Garamond Variable (with Cormorant Garamond, Georgia, and serif fallbacks)
**Interface Font:** Manrope Variable (with Manrope, Segoe UI, Arial, and sans-serif fallbacks)
**Arabic Display Font:** Noto Naskh Arabic (with serif fallback)  
**Arabic Body Font:** IBM Plex Sans Arabic (with Segoe UI, Tahoma, Arial, and sans-serif fallbacks)

**Character:** The shell uses confident Manrope at display scale, making the proposition modern and direct. Cormorant belongs to the invitation object and authored ceremonial moments. Arabic display uses Noto Naskh Arabic with open leading and no forced Latin tracking; Arabic interface copy uses IBM Plex Sans Arabic.

### Hierarchy

- **Hero:** Heavy Manrope with tight leading and tracking; the first fold keeps the headline to two explicit lines in both language modes.
- **Invitation Display:** Cormorant Garamond for names and crafted invitation moments, with compressed leading for short compositions.
- **Arabic Display:** Noto Naskh Arabic with native shaping, open leading, and zero tracking.
- **Body:** Manrope or IBM Plex Sans Arabic with generous leading and a practical measure near 52 characters in the hero.
- **Label:** Compact, bold interface text for controls, preview labels, prices, metadata, and state.

**The Two-Line Promise Rule.** The first-fold proposition stays two lines; do not let responsive copy expand into a third display line.

**The Native Arabic Rule.** Never simulate Arabic hierarchy with Latin negative tracking or Latin display metrics.

## Layout

The first viewport is a balanced asymmetrical split: a concise copy column and a larger interactive invitation stage. Desktop uses a minimum 360px copy region and 520px visual region, with fluid outer padding from 22px to 96px. The stage is at least 570px high and a three-step workflow band completes the fold.

Below the fold, broad porcelain, lilac-soft, white, and one aubergine feature field alternate. Collections use a dominant preview plus a responsive grid; workflow and feature groups use rules rather than floating cards. Pricing is intentionally compact: 48–72px section padding, 16px plan gaps, and concise plan rows keep comparison useful.

At 980px the hero becomes two equal flexible columns and the stage reduces to 500px. At 760px the header simplifies, the hero stacks, actions become full width, gutters become 18px, the stage remains 480px, and workflow becomes one ruled column. Invitation content uses a 1080px outer measure; message, countdown, and RSVP cap at 820px, while schedules cap at 920px. At 700px invitation gutters become 16px and forms collapse to one column.

**The First-Fold Demonstration Rule.** Copy explains the value, the invitation proves it, and the three-step workflow closes the fold.

**The Continuous Lilac Rule.** Do not insert a hard white slab between hero copy and invitation stage; the first viewport is one lilac material field.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is material and selective. The porcelain invitation on photographed lilac silk receives the strongest ambient separation; template cards and operational overlays receive quieter aubergine-tinted shadows. Hairlines and tonal fields carry ordinary hierarchy. Still is intentionally flat, while dark invitation worlds may use deeper ambient falloff.

### Shadow Vocabulary

- **Atelier Stage:** Broad aubergine-tinted separation (`0 24px 70px rgba(67, 40, 70, .16)`) for the physical hero scene.
- **Primary Action:** Compact olive-tinted lift (`0 12px 28px rgba(83, 70, 32, .16)`) that grows on hover.
- **Template Rest:** Restrained depth (`0 14px 38px rgba(38, 23, 42, .10)`) that becomes deeper only on hover.
- **Invitation Surface:** Action-tinted ambient depth inherited from the active invitation world.

**The Physical Material Rule.** Use shadow when a surface behaves like paper, silk-mounted art, or an overlay; use tone and hairlines for ordinary grouping.

## Shapes

The shell uses tailored corners: 13px actions, 15px cards, and 16px stages. Preview thumbnails and overlays use tighter 9–10px corners. Inside invitations, controls and ceremony surfaces return to precise 2–4px corners so each world reads like authored print. Circles are reserved for circular marks and atmospheric geometry.

**The Tailored Corner Rule.** Shell surfaces may feel softly upholstered; invitation controls remain precise and print-like.

## Components

### Buttons

- **Primary Creation:** 54px high, chartreuse fill, dark aubergine ink, 13px corners, and bold label type. Hover brightens, lifts 2px, and deepens the shadow.
- **Secondary:** 54px high, transparent, aubergine text, restrained border, and 13px corners.
- **Invitation Action:** 56px high with 3px corners; background, text, hover, and focus come from the active invitation world.
- **Focus:** Shell controls use a visible violet outline. Invitation controls use the active world's gold/focus role with 4px offset.

### Chips

- **Template Swatches:** Image buttons with a 9px crop, white border, and shallow shadow. Selection uses a chartreuse border plus aubergine outer ring; hover lifts 3px.
- **RSVP Choices:** 44px minimum height, 2–3px corners, paper background, and local border. Selection becomes the local action color with local action ink.

### Cards / Containers

- **Invitation Stage:** A 16px clipped frame containing the production silk-and-porcelain scene, semantic preview label, invitation control, and three swatches.
- **Template Cards:** 15px corners with imagery dominant; rest and hover depth follow the template shadow vocabulary.
- **Plan Cards:** 15px corners, 1px atelier line, white ground; the featured plan uses a lilac tint and stronger aubergine border without oversized promotion.
- **Ceremony Surfaces:** 3px corners, local paper and ink, restrained borders, and palette-aware ambient depth.

### Inputs / Fields

- **Style:** Invitation inputs are at least 50px high, use the world's field/paper color, local ink, and local border.
- **Focus:** A clear local gold/focus outline remains visible in every invitation world.
- **Density:** Guest name and meal fields use a two-to-one desktop grid and collapse to one column at 700px.

### Navigation

- **Header:** 82px translucent porcelain bar with aubergine text and one hairline; the recognizable wordmark remains while emblem duplication is suppressed on home.
- **Mobile:** At 760px, navigation and the header creation action leave the crowded bar while locale access remains.

### Interactive Invitation Preview

The stage uses the porcelain-on-lilac-silk production raster as material truth. Names, date, selection, preview label, and open action remain semantic HTML. Three authored preview images switch the specimen without baking interface copy into the scene.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:

- **Do** keep pale lilac continuous across the first fold and let the invitation stage carry the visual weight.
- **Do** reserve chartreuse for creation, selection, and decisive states.
- **Do** preserve a two-line hero proposition in English and Arabic.
- **Do** keep pricing compact and information-dense after expressive material sections.
- **Do** let each of the six invitation worlds own its palette, image treatment, composition, and entrance while sharing accessible RSVP structure.
- **Do** keep UI text, state, crop, radius, and motion in code; imagery supplies atmosphere, not product claims.

### Don't:

- **Don't** revive celestial, observatory, copper-orbit, or near-black plum styling in the shared shell.
- **Don't** turn the six invitations into one shell with six accent-color swaps.
- **Don't** cover the page in chartreuse or use it as a general background.
- **Don't** place text-critical interface meaning inside generated imagery.
- **Don't** force Latin tracking, alignment assumptions, or line metrics onto Arabic.
- **Don't** fabricate testimonials, customer counts, delivery capabilities, or performance evidence.
